# Choose one police architecture before downloading mods

<p className="guide-lead">Your choice controls the police framework, MDT, dispatch, voice systems, dependencies, and troubleshooting instructions used throughout the guide.</p>

![Diagram comparing the free modern, paid realism, and legacy Sentinel paths](/img/sentinel-build/build-paths.svg)

:::danger The most important compatibility rule
The modern path uses **Policing Redefined**. Do not leave **Stop The Ped**, **Ultimate Backup**, or **CompuLite** active beside it. Those plugins overlap in stops, arrests, backup, records, menus, and entity handling.
:::

## Quick recommendation

<div className="guide-decision">
  <h3>New to GTA modding?</h3>
  <p>Start with the <strong>Free Modern Build</strong>. It teaches the same safe installation method and modern architecture without requiring paid access. Move to paid voice and Nexus features only after the free foundation is stable.</p>

## Side-by-side comparison

| Question | Free Modern Build | Paid Maximum-Realism Build | Legacy Alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best for | First-time installers and no-cost builds | Natural voice, premium Nexus workflow, maximum immersion | Existing STP users who do not want to migrate |
| Police framework | Policing Redefined | Policing Redefined | Stop The Ped + Ultimate Backup |
| MDT | NexusMDT Lite or one compatible free records option | NexusMDT FULL | CompuLite |
| Dispatch | AIDispatch or a supported free option | Nexus Dispatch | GrammarPolice or menu-based dispatch |
| Pedestrian voice | NPCI phrase recognition | NPCAI open-ended AI conversation | NPCI STP edition or menu interaction |
| Cost | Core route can be completed without paid memberships | Requires creator purchases or memberships | Mostly free, depending on optional additions |
| Complexity | Moderate | Highest | Moderate, but older architecture |
| Sentinel recommendation | **Best starting point** | Best final realism target | Keep separate; do not mix with modern stack |

## Legacy Alternative

The legacy pages remain for users who intentionally run Stop The Ped, Ultimate Backup, and CompuLite. They are not a cheaper version of the modern path; they are a different architecture.

Use the legacy route only when:

- Your current build already depends on STP/UB/CompuLite.
- You do not want to rebuild around Policing Redefined.
- A required plugin explicitly supports the legacy ecosystem but not PR.

[Read the migration guide →](./migrate-legacy-to-modern)

## What “paid” means

Project Sentinel never redistributes protected files. Paid guides provide installation order, configuration, test gates, and troubleshooting, then direct the reader to the creator's official purchase or entitlement source. Prices, access terms, and delivered versions can change.

## Rules shared by every path

<ol className="guide-steps">
  <li><strong>Confirm GTA V Legacy</strong><p>Enhanced and Legacy use different binaries and compatibility states. Do not assume a guide for one applies to the other.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Version-match the core</strong><p>GTA, LSPDFR, RPH, ScriptHookV, GameConfig, and major plugins must support the same title update.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Keep only one owner for each job</strong><p>One police framework, one main records workflow, one dispatch workflow, and non-conflicting push-to-talk keys.</p></li>
  <li><strong>Test before expanding</strong><p>Pass the stage-specific in-game test before adding uniforms, vehicles, callouts, graphics, or sirens.</p></li>
</ol>
